4-axis pulley

Rotary Pocket roughing, then Rotary Parallel and Spiral.

Rotary Pocket behaves like Parallel, so it's good on shallow surfaces. Rotary Contour behaves like 3D Contour, so it's the better choice on steep surfaces. Rotary Parallel can sit in between the two in some areas, but it's mainly a surfacing-only toolpath.

5-axis Y pipe flange

Various 3D surfacing strategies with 5-axis enabled. We went through the primary 5-axis modes, then how the additional settings layer more control on top of them, including automatic and guided collision avoidance.

We also covered 3+3 roughing, and how to apply and control it.

Electronics enclosure

Toolpath modifications, and multi-axis finishing with circular segment tools.

Surface quality and toolpath quality

The quality of the surfaces you machine has a direct effect on the quality of the toolpath, and how much it matters depends on how the toolpath reads the model. We grouped the 3D and 5-axis toolpaths that way:

Offline work and what's coming

For any job whose data can't be stored on Autodesk's servers, the AirGap add-in forces Fusion into offline mode, blocks cloud saves, and has you export locally instead.

Read the limitations in its README before relying on it. Fusion still has to go online every 14 days to validate its licence, so plan that work inside the window.

  • Repo
    airgap-fusion

    Open-source Fusion add-in by Infab Softworks.

Autodesk have also announced local data control for Fusion for Manufacturing. You'll be able to choose, design by design, to keep data under your own control while still working in Fusion. It's planned for the Fusion Insider Program from 17 November, with general availability targeted for 19 January. Those are Autodesk's dates and they may change.
